# About Me

I am a Master's student specialising in Health Technologies and Data Science. My work focuses on applying machine learning and deep learning techniques to medical and healthcare data. I have a background in Medical Imaging hence, I am naturally inclined towards the application of AI in medical imaging. 

## Technical Skills

- **Neuroimaging & MRI Signal Processing**: FSL, FreeSurfer, MONAI, Docker, WSL
- **Data Analysis & Visualisation**: Python, Excel, SQL
- **Healthcare Systems**: PACS, RIS, DICOM Standards, Electronic Health Records (EHR)
- **Machine Learning**: PyTorch, scikit-learn

## Current Focus

I am currently working on developing a DL pipeline for early Parkinson's disease detection using raw DWI signals instead of usual DTI metrics and benchmarking them to see what information is lost when we summarise an entire DWI signal into mathematical values like FA or MD.
